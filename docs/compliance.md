# Evidence rules and sources

Checked 21 October 2026. This is an administrative evidence checklist, not a payroll calculator or an employment-law decision system. The employer and its qualified advisers decide pay, applicable minimum wages, awards, agreements, discrimination questions and communication wording. The program does not certify compliance.

| Rule | Record check | Why and source |
|---|---|---|
| BASELINE | An annual salary has a source reference before submission. | Local control supporting accurate agreed pay records. [Employment New Zealand](https://www.employment.govt.nz/pay-and-hours/pay-and-wages/paying-employees) explains that pay is agreed and must meet applicable requirements. |
| NZ-AGREEMENT | An approved NZ change without agreement evidence remains on the chase list. | The same official page explains agreement and good faith. Recording an evidence reference does not establish that the agreement is valid. We require agreement evidence for every jurisdiction before handover as a local control. |
| AU-PAY-REVIEW | An Australian submission remains flagged until a human employment-policy review reference is supplied. | [Fair Work Ombudsman](https://www.fairwork.gov.au/pay-and-wages/pay-secrecy) explains employee rights to share or withhold their own pay information and restrictions on pay secrecy terms. A reviewer checks actual wording and applicable pay rules outside this program. The reference is evidence of review, not an automated legal finding. |
| POLICY | A submitted proposal needs a human policy-review reference before approval. | Local governance control. It covers applicable pay requirements, basis of annual salary, equal-treatment considerations and agreed terms. The program cannot perform these checks from salary amounts alone. |
| BAND-EXCEPTION | An out-of-band proposal needs a separate exception reference before approval. | Employer policy, not a statutory minimum or a market benchmark. Bands are supplied and approved by the employer. |

## Local controls

Independent reviewer labels, reserved budgets, integer cents, separate currencies, explicit import reconciliation and immutable finalisation are design controls. They are not legal requirements attributed to the sources above. Labels are not authentication and the database owner can alter the records. A shared installation needs authenticated identities, scoped permissions and a separate immutable audit service if the employer requires one.

No minimum wage number is hard-coded. Annual salary alone cannot establish hourly minimum compliance, award classification, overtime, allowances, holiday pay or pension obligations. This tool does not calculate those amounts and never connects to payroll.

Protect the employer's dataset and limit generated documents to authorised recipients. Do not place an employee pay-secrecy clause in any document. Protecting other people's personal information does not remove an employee's rights concerning their own pay.
